# Project Charter: AI-Driven Circular Freight Booking System

## 1. Project Overview
This project aims to transform the freight booking experience by integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Circular Economy principles. The goal is to reduce logistics waste, optimize vehicle utility, and provide a sustainable transport ecosystem for Shippers and Carriers.

## 2. Mission Statement
To democratize advanced logistics technology for SMEs and promote environmental sustainability through intelligent resource optimization.

## 3. Core Objectives
- **AI-Powered Matching:** Implement predictive algorithms to reduce empty backhauls by 40%.
- **Circular Integration:** Track and optimize the reuse of transport assets and packaging.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Reduce carbon footprint and operational costs for all stakeholders.

## 4. Key Stakeholders
- **Shippers:** SMEs and manufacturing units requiring transparent, cost-effective logistics.
- **Carriers:** Professional transport providers seeking to maximize vehicle utility.
- **Project Team:** Developers, Data Scientists, and Supply Chain experts.

## 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)
- Reduction in "Empty Miles" per vehicle.
- Platform adoption rate among regional SMEs.
- Accuracy of demand forecasting models.
